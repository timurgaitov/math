## Covered

- Recall from 0002: choose-steps from "b even and b | c" — clean, went
  unprompted all the way to proving c even (closure line supplied when
  asked). 0 | 0 justified correctly by definition. Statement vs. open
  sentence: verdict and quantifier-edit right, but the term **free

- **Direct proof** named and formalized, extracted from the student's own
  warm-up proof. Two-layer picture: logical skeleton (arbitrary → assume P
  → derive Q → conclude ∀) vs. working skeleton (unpack via choose →
  compute → deliver via exhibit). The layer split was prompted by the
  student's own correct objection that "choose" can't apply to an abstract
  P. → [notes/direct-proof.md](../notes/direct-proof.md)
- Skeleton-first exercise: a | b, a | c ⇒ a | (b+c)
  ([0005](../exercises/0005-divisibility-of-sum.md), solved — setup and
  obligation stated before algebra, clean).
- **Contrapositive**: equivalence verified by hand truth table; "n² even ⇒
  n even" attempted directly first (on purpose) to feel the wall, then
  flipped and proved ([0006](../exercises/0006-square-even-implies-even.md),
  solved). Parity fact introduced (not-even ⇔ odd, granted until division
  with remainder). → [notes/contrapositive.md](../notes/contrapositive.md)
- Technique-choice heuristic discussed: look where each version puts the
  raw material; composite-object assumptions with simple-object goals are
  the contrapositive tell.
